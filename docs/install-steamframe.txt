FrameTube for Steam Frame
=========================
Watch YouTube and YouTube VR videos right on your Steam Frame, no PC needed.
(For the smoothest 5K/8K VR videos, the Windows version streamed from a PC is better.)

These steps follow SteamOS's Desktop Mode, the same as on a Steam Deck. Menu names on
the Steam Frame may differ slightly.


INSTALL (about 5 minutes)
-------------------------
1. Switch the Frame to Desktop Mode
   (Steam menu > Power > Switch to Desktop).

2. Open the web browser, download the FrameTube-SteamFrame zip file,
   then open your Downloads folder.

3. Right-click the zip file and choose "Extract" (or "Extract archive here").

4. In the extracted folder, right-click "FrameTube.arm64" and choose "Add to Steam".

5. Switch back to Gaming Mode (the "Return to Gaming Mode" icon on the desktop).
   FrameTube is now in your library, under "Non-Steam".

6. Start it. The first time, FrameTube downloads its video helpers by itself
   (about 200 MB). Just wait for the bar to fill up. This only happens once.


HOW TO USE IT
-------------
  Point and pull the TRIGGER ........ press buttons, pick a video
  B / Y (or the Menu button) ........ show / hide the menu
  A / X ............................. pause / play
  Thumbstick left / right ........... skip back / forward 10 seconds
  GRIP while pointing at the screen . grab and move the screen
  GRIP with both hands .............. pull apart to make the screen bigger
  GRIP while pointing at the menu ... move the menu (both hands: make it bigger / smaller)
  Thumbstick click .................. re-center everything in front of you
                                      (also calls the fidget toy back)

Settings lets you choose what you see of yourself (hands, controllers, both or nothing),
and whether the fidget toy floats back, stays where you leave it, or falls to the floor.

Tap a "Discover" topic to find something to watch without typing. After a search, press
"+ Save" to add it as a topic; "Edit" removes topics (Settings can bring the originals back).
Lists keep loading more videos as you scroll down.
Videos remember where you stopped: find them under "Continue watching".
Want to watch without internet? Press "Download" on a search result or while a video plays;
it shows up under "Downloads"
(press "Delete" there to remove it).


SEND VIDEOS BETWEEN THE FRAME AND A PC
--------------------------------------
With FrameTube on a PC too (same Wi-Fi), the two find each other by themselves:
- On the PC, Settings > "Save downloads to" > "Steam Frame": the PC downloads (faster),
  then sends the video here over Wi-Fi the next time FrameTube is open on both.
  (Leave FrameTube running on the PC while you open it here on the Frame.)
- In "Downloads", "Send to PC" / "Send to Frame" sends a copy of a saved video.
The first time a device sends, you're asked "Allow videos from ...?": press Allow.
Turn this off with Settings > "Receive videos from other devices".


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- A video stutters: choose a lower "Quality" (4K is the sweet spot on the headset; Auto does this for you).
- A video won't play: try again (FrameTube updates its YouTube helper by itself).
- Videos never play: open Settings and press "Repair".
- Age-restricted videos, your subscriptions, Watch later: open Settings and press "Sign in".
  A browser window opens; sign in to YouTube there and it closes by itself.
  Do this in Desktop Mode, and install Firefox first if you don't have it (Discover store).
- The 3D looks "inside out": turn on "Swap eyes".

FORGOT THE PARENTAL PIN?
------------------------
Close FrameTube, then delete the file "settings.cfg" in FrameTube's data folder:
  Windows:      %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\FrameTube
                (paste that into the address bar of a File Explorer window)
  Steam Frame:  ~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/FrameTube   (in Desktop Mode)
This removes the PIN and resets FrameTube's settings. Your watch history is kept.
