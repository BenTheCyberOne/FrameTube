FrameTube for Steam Frame
=========================
Watch YouTube and YouTube VR videos right on your Steam Frame, no PC needed.
(For the smoothest 5K/8K VR videos, the Windows version streamed from a PC is better.)

These steps follow SteamOS's Desktop Mode, the same as on a Steam Deck. Menu names on
the Steam Frame may differ slightly.


INSTALL (about 5 minutes)
-------------------------
1. Switch the Frame to Desktop Mode
   (Steam menu > Power > Switch to Desktop).

2. Open the web browser, download the FrameTube-SteamFrame zip file,
   then open your Downloads folder.

3. Right-click the zip file and choose "Extract" (or "Extract archive here").

4. In the extracted folder, right-click "FrameTube.arm64" and choose "Add to Steam".

5. Switch back to Gaming Mode (the "Return to Gaming Mode" icon on the desktop).
   FrameTube is now in your library, under "Non-Steam".

6. Start it. The first time, FrameTube downloads its video helpers by itself
   (about 200 MB). Just wait for the bar to fill up. This only happens once.


HOW TO USE IT
-------------
  Point and pull the TRIGGER ........ press buttons, pick a video
  B / Y (or the Menu button) ........ show / hide the menu
  A / X ............................. pause / play
  Thumbstick left / right ........... skip back / forward 10 seconds
  GRIP while pointing at the screen . grab and move the screen
  GRIP with both hands .............. pull apart to make the screen bigger
  Thumbstick click .................. re-center everything in front of you

Tap a "Discover" topic to find something to watch without typing.
Videos remember where you stopped: find them under "Continue watching".


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- A video stutters: choose a lower "Quality" (4K is the sweet spot on the headset).
- A video won't play: try again (FrameTube updates its YouTube helper by itself).
- Videos never play: open Settings and press "Repair".
- Age-restricted videos, your subscriptions, Watch later: open Settings > YouTube account.
  Sign in to YouTube in Firefox in Desktop Mode first, then pick Firefox there.
- The 3D looks "inside out": turn on "Swap eyes".
