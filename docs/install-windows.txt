FrameTube for Windows
=====================
Watch YouTube and YouTube VR videos in your Steam Frame, streamed from this PC.
(The PC does the heavy lifting, so even 8K VR videos play smoothly.)


INSTALL (about 2 minutes)
-------------------------
1. Right-click the FrameTube zip file and choose "Extract All...", then "Extract".

2. Open the extracted folder and double-click FrameTube.exe.

   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC":
   click "More info", then "Run anyway". (This appears because FrameTube is a free
   app that isn't registered with Microsoft. It's safe to run.)

3. The first time it opens, FrameTube downloads its video helpers by itself
   (about 300 MB). Just wait for the bar to fill up. This only happens once.


WATCH IN YOUR STEAM FRAME
-------------------------
1. Connect your Steam Frame to this PC (start SteamVR, as you would for any PC VR game).
2. Open FrameTube. It appears in the headset automatically.

Tip: put FrameTube in your Steam library so you can start it from inside VR:
   In Steam: Games menu > "Add a Non-Steam Game to My Library..." > Browse...
   > pick FrameTube.exe > "Add Selected Programs".


HOW TO USE IT (in VR)
---------------------
  Point and pull the TRIGGER ........ press buttons, pick a video
  B / Y (or the Menu button) ........ show / hide the menu
  A / X ............................. pause / play
  Thumbstick left / right ........... skip back / forward 10 seconds
  GRIP while pointing at the screen . grab and move the screen
  GRIP with both hands .............. pull apart to make the screen bigger
  GRIP while pointing at the menu ... move the menu (both hands: make it bigger / smaller)
  Thumbstick click .................. re-center everything in front of you
                                      (also calls the fidget toy back)

Settings lets you choose what you see of yourself (hands, controllers, both or nothing),
and whether the fidget toy floats back, stays where you leave it, or falls to the floor.

Tap a "Discover" topic to find something to watch without typing. After a search, press
"+ Save" to add it as a topic; "Edit" removes topics (Settings can bring the originals back).
Lists keep loading more videos as you scroll down.
Videos remember where you stopped: find them under "Continue watching".
Want to watch without internet? Press "Download" on a search result or while a video plays;
it shows up under "Downloads"
(press "Delete" there to remove it).

Without a headset FrameTube opens in a normal window: click to use the menu,
hold the right mouse button and move to look around, Tab shows/hides the menu.


SEND VIDEOS TO YOUR STEAM FRAME
-------------------------------
Want to take videos with you and watch them on the Frame without the PC?
1. Install FrameTube on the Frame too (see its own guide), on the same Wi-Fi as this PC.
2. Open FrameTube on both. In Settings, "Save downloads to" now offers "Steam Frame".
3. Downloads then happen on the PC (faster) and are sent to the Frame over Wi-Fi.
   If the Frame's FrameTube isn't open, they wait and go the next time both are open.
   (While you're streaming from the PC, the Frame's own FrameTube can't be open. So leave
   FrameTube running on the PC, stop streaming, and start FrameTube on the Frame itself:
   the waiting videos arrive within a minute.)
   In "Downloads", "Send to Frame" sends a copy of something you've already saved.
The first time, the Frame asks "Allow videos from <your PC>?": press Allow.
Windows may ask whether FrameTube can use the network: allow it on private networks.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- A video won't play: try again (FrameTube updates its YouTube helper by itself),
  or choose a lower "Quality" (Auto picks the best one your PC can play smoothly).
- Videos never play: open Settings and press "Repair".
- Age-restricted videos, your subscriptions, Watch later: open Settings and press "Sign in".
  A browser window opens; sign in to YouTube there. Firefox closes by itself;
  with Chrome or Edge, close the window yourself when you're signed in.
  If you're wearing the headset, the window is on the PC's screen (or in SteamVR's desktop view).
- The 3D looks "inside out": press "3D eyes" (under the player) to swap them.

FORGOT THE PARENTAL PIN?
------------------------
Close FrameTube, then delete the file "settings.cfg" in FrameTube's data folder:
  Windows:      %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\FrameTube
                (paste that into the address bar of a File Explorer window)
  Steam Frame:  ~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/FrameTube   (in Desktop Mode)
This removes the PIN and resets FrameTube's settings. Your watch history is kept.
